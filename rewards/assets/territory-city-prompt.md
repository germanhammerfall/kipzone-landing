One irregular closed running route follows streets and encloses several blocks, bright KipZone lime #D4E600 edge with controlled soft glow and a few luminous corner nodes, translucent lime territory overlay preserving all city detail beneath. Subtle cyan starting marker. The scene is a believable city terrain patch, not an extruded polygon, not a floating sci-fi platform. Composition landscape 4:3, entire closed contour fully visible with 12 percent breathing room, centered. Dark charcoal buildings; clear local building contrast and bright readable boundary at small dashboard sizes. Surrounding city streets fade softly into a genuinely transparent alpha background, so it can overlay dark cards. Only city and route: absolutely NO typography, labels, numbers, slogans, logos, flags, coins, wallets, people, runner character or UI. Do not copy the screenshot's headings or phone interface. High fidelity cinematic city render, mature running game aesthetic, restrained glow, no purple or pink.
